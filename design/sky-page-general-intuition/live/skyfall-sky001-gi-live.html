How SKY-001 beats Prompt-Based Methods Step-By-Step

Stage 1

Parallel Explorations Through World Modeling

Our model converts a 2D sketch or text prompt into several rough 3D assets at once. This lets you instantly explore different visual directions and pick the best foundation before diving into details.

Stage 2

Recursive Diffusion-Based 3D Decomposition

Once selected, it breaks down the high-level designs into smaller, individual parts, from large structural pieces all the way down to tiny screws and fasteners. By working layer by layer, our model keeps complex designs organized and easy to build, excelling in long horizon planning.

Stage 3

Multi-step 2d to 3D Latent Action World Model

It brings each part to life by recreating how a real designer works step-by-step. It starts by drawing precise 2D sketches stroke-by-stroke, then stretches, cuts, or shapes those sketches into finished 3D objects.

Stage 4

3D Construction World Model

Finally, it assembles all the parts, layer by layer again, in hierarchical order into a complete 3D assembly that you can visualize immediately in standard CAD software

Reinventing the physical economy with world models

Today, SKY-001 is a hierarchical visual world model that plans and designs the assembly of engineered objects. By pairing it with a vision-language-action (VLA) agent in the next phase, we transition from plan generation to physical execution. Beyond manufacturing, SKY-001 is designed to become the underlying blueprint for complex physical design, spanning PCB design, Aerospace engineering, Energy and Power systems, Protein folding, Molecule design, Computer Use Agents, Enterprise Software and beyond.

Wherever structure, constraints, and sequence matter, SKY-001 plans and powers the build.
